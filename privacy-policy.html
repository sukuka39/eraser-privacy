Eraser — Privacy Policy

App: Eraser — Homework & Worksheet Scanner (package com.silgi.scanner)
Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Eraser cleans photos of homework and worksheets entirely on your device. Your photos and scanned pages are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics of our own.

Photos and scanned pages

  • When you take a photo or pick one from your gallery, it is processed on your device (page detection, perspective correction, shadow and handwriting removal, text recognition).
  • Pages you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.
  • A page leaves your device only when you choose to share, print or export it. It then goes to the app or printer you pick, and that app's own policy applies.

Camera

The camera is opened through your phone's own camera app, only when you tap the scan button. Eraser does not record video or audio.

Text recognition (Google ML Kit)

Eraser uses Google ML Kit on-device text recognition to find printed text it should keep. Images and recognised text are processed on the device and are not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical diagnostics about how the feature performs, such as processing time, image format and size, and device and app version. Google uses this for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and ML Kit terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, location or any account data.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.

Children

Eraser can be used by students. Because the app collects no personal data and has no accounts, ads or chat, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
